# Design notes

Pages in this folder record design positions the framework has adopted, each with its date, parties, the repos it touches, what it means for the build, and open questions. They are written for a reader who does not share the authors' context; the status block at the top of each page is the handshake.

Positions are dated and are not edited in place. A changed position gets a new page or a dated section.
