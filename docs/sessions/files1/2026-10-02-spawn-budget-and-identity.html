1Where it started

Jerry, after reading the delivered 2026-10-01 docs: agents write to a shared ledger all agents read; a new container sees that a predecessor completed three jobs and learns that if it cannot match that, it gets fewer spawns. He called it "enslaving math" and asked for the logic.

2Mechanism, then pushback

Receipts signed per lineage key are the history. The step-zero read also reads the fleet weights. The consequence is a spawn budget per lineage.

Pushback from inside the idea: "most jobs" is a Goodhart target; score the stitch, not throughput; agents read receipts, never prose; the scorer must sit outside the pool.
